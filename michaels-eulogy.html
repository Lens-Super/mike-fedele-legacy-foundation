Michael's Eulogy

Written and Delivered by His Sister, Stephanie

The hardest part of writing this
Besides having to do it at all
Is trying to decide where to even begin
(This is the part where Michael would
    grab me alcohol)

So how do I even begin to tell you
The kind of man that Michael was
A vibrant, hilarious, life of the party
Even without a buzz

The kind that would sing at the top of his
    lungs
At karaoke at a bar
And not care at all what anyone thought
Because he was a friggin rock star

The animal lover that never met
A dog that wasn't obsessed
He even had the ducks respond to his
   quack
You couldn't help but be impressed

He truly was a jack of all trades
And I'd say a master of some
But honestly everywhere that he went
He just made it that much more fun.

A professional gambler,
A salesman too
Photo Booth owner
Just to name a few

A construction worker,
For the best company there is
Who loved the work but, that 7 am start
He tried HARD to get noon to be his

A collegiate football player whose
Enthusiasm exceeded his skill
But as the soccer goalie or on the
    wrestling mat, 
That’s where he made his kills

Thinking about my brother
I must honestly convey
You'll bring up his name and never hear
Anything bad to say

You'll most likely get an immediate laugh
A Fedele story of their own
Of some dumb shit he’s done or said or
    joked
His effect on this world has been shown

I could be a cliche and say he lit up the
    room
But that wouldn't do it justice
The amount of people's lives he touched
Almost as much as he said the word
    "F*#@" is

He had such comedic timing
A sense of humor like no other
He was so quick witted and that sarcasm
We all know he got from his mother

And his stubbornness and loyalty
He got from his dad
And even when they butted heads
No greater love was had

A metro fashionista, 
And mom’s Mickey Moo
Who always liked to plan his outfits
To pop those baby blues

Unless of course he was staying in
It would be quite a different dress
He lived in his bathrobe and wore it proudly
Definitely something you might not have
    guessed

He truly loved taking photos
Capturing so many moments and treasures
And the selfies that we've come across
Way more than we want to measure

And when we were on the job site
And I wanted to get a pic
He said wait let me pose with one of the
    tools
Or behind the truck so I don't look thick

He also was always recording videos
He got every Happy Birthday
Of him singing super loud and off key
Followed by a drawn out Yaaaaaay

And don't forget about that toothpick
He always had in his teeth
Or when he was the best man
And straight up roasted Kim and Keith

I don't know if it's possible to count
How many meals we ate
Where we all sat down at the table and
Michael paced around with a paper plate

He was always on the move
You'd never see him sit still
DJing, cracking jokes, busting balls
That was the way that he'd chill

If you had to wait for him
When Michael was in the shower
You'd know you weren't going to leave
For at the very least an hour

But if you called to check on him
Don't worry he would say
I just got on 95
I'm definitely on my way

But he'd still be in that shower
Or should I say the recording booth
The walls would shake as he would sing
That's the God's honest truth

The radio would blast
While he'd belt out a number
And then a random F Bomb
Would wake you from your slumber

We'd laughingly say he was tourettes
    adjacent
There was absolutely zero restraint
He'd splatter those curses into any
    conversation
Like Jackson Pollock did paint

And I know I've read in an article
That its the most intelligent people who
    swear
So Michael could have been in Mensa
But I think one of their requirements was
    hair

That verse could bring me to two different
    things
That we truly loved about Mike
His smart, competitive brain
Or his failing baldness fight

Tell me you've seen anyone else
That could put a fedora on
And not look like a complete douchebag
I bet you can't name one!

But he rocked the shit out of all of his hats
A cap, a Jeff, a fedora,
And bandanas for days, and cut off sleeves
Just became a part of his aura

Now back to that brain of his
And his sense of competition
Catching up on jeopardy with him
Became a bit of a tradition

We'd get more than a little intense
When any trivia was at play
They never put us on the same team
Or it would have been a runaway

It went as far back as school times
And I know he's hating me for this
But I just have to bring up
My SAT score was higher than his

I'll never forget when I told him
And I assume he called me a bitch
But he was the proudest and most
    supportive big brother
And I was so proud to be his sis

It takes a special kind of man
Who was never too cool or sly
To end every single phone call
With love ya, Bye

I'll never forget the one time
We hung up with love ya bye
And someone had assumed
It was my boyfriend on the line

I told them it was my brother
And they were incredulous
I realized then how lucky I was
Not all families have a love like this

I remember when he'd call me
And Mark would always say
Oh is that Michael, this will be hours
Then he'd walk away

I am so grateful for those calls
Of catching up and shooting the shit
And occasionally naming more tunes
    than I could
Maybe more than I'd like to admit

And no one knew how to pick out
A Pump up song quite like him
To a mud run, a wedding, a sporting event
Didn't matter, we were getting a WIN

He taught his god daughter how to drive
And we'll never forgive him for that
   transgression
Though her road rage isn’t nearly as bad
He almost fought someone in Chaz’s
   funeral procession

He'd be there to take Chazzy to football
   practice
Driving all the way from Delco
And to work some photo booth events
   with him
Rapping Eminem like a damn pro

Club and Freestyle Music,
He’d listen and bond with Kim
And was always there to take her friends
   to dances
How very selfless of him

No matter what, he looked out for Keith
At North and then beyond
They went from fighting in their bunk beds
To an unbreakable, brotherly bond

He’d pop over to the house 
If something needed to be fixed
Or to help you hook up tech
Or give his best sports betting picks

You always knew if you needed him
No matter what he'd be there
Even if it was 6 hours later
“So much traffic guys, I swear”

What time is Christmas dinner this year?
Well the real time or what we told you
We had to pad it to Mike standard time
We'd tell him 12 when it was actually 2

And he always gave the best presents
So personal, thoughtful, Just great
And the most hilarious birthday cards
Even if they were a month or two late

And the fun, noisy, loud gifts 
For KJ, Gianna and Vin
Super soakers and nerf guns
Enough for everyone to shoot to win

And while our hearts have been utterly
Ripped out from our chest
We have so so many memories 
And I’m grateful, they’re the best

I'll never forget when mom saw a mouse
Dad sent him to "rescue" her and me
He came in singing at the top of his lungs
M-I-C-K-E-Y  M-O-U-S-E

And that day Mom and Dad were in church
Wearing an old school Nextel
When a chirp came across from the jobsite
Of just straight obscenities and yells

Or the time on vacation down the shore
On the go kart track
He got air down the hill, went completely
   off course
We could barely get him back!

And that nervous laughter that he'd get
When he was in trouble or something was
   wrong
Mom and dad would tell him to stop
But the chuckle went on and on

How about when he’d call to say 
He couldn’t drive his car
Well what happened mike? Well I was late
   for class and it just got booted
No idea why, how bizarre! 

Or when he and Bree came over to listen
   to songs
To plan my wedding playlist 
And we sang so loud and Bree and I
   rapped
And he couldn’t have been more impressed

When we suffered a devastating loss
And didn't know how we would go on
He stepped up for his family
Niece and nephew, now like daughter and son

And we must take comfort in knowing
The many that welcomed him home
I know Chaz was there with him
To make sure he wasn’t alone

We’ve all cried a ton from this immeasurable loss
Of a son, brother, uncle and friend
But we’ll smile and laugh even more than
   those tears
Because our love for him is without end

And the impact he left on all that he met
Will be felt every single day
He’ll be with us in nature, with animals, a
   rainbow
A song that will randomly play

So please when you think of Michael
And how he was taken far far too soon
Try not to let the heartbreak
Overtake the room

Be grateful that we were blessed 
with someone like him in our lives
And we who knew and loved him
Will make sure his memory survives

For even though we are broken
We are truly lucky because
We got to experience this once in a lifetime 
Kind of man Michael was

Genuine Love

Patience wasn't typically Michael's strong suit.

But when Snickers got older and their walks down his parents' driveway became slower, Michael slowed down with her. He never rushed her. He let her take her time, however long that took.

Because genuine love isn't always about grand gestures. Sometimes it's something as simple as slowing down for someone who can't keep up anymore.

It's the kind of love that shows up, listens, waits, and stays.
